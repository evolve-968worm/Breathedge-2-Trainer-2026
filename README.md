## 🎮 About the Game

Breathedge 2 is a single-player space survival adventure developed by RedRuins Softworks and published by HypeTrain Digital. It entered Steam Early Access on August 31, 2026, combining exploration, crafting, resource management, vehicle control, crew management, and action gameplay in a humorous retro-futuristic universe.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Breathedge 2**. This trainer provides optional assistance with Breathedge 2's core survival systems, including oxygen management, health, resource collection, crafting, exploration, vehicles, and combat. It is designed to reduce repetitive resource pressure while preserving the game's story and exploration structure.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed experience, faster crafting and upgrades, easier exploration, or additional protection during the game's Early Access development period.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the game's normal movement and vehicle controls. Activate oxygen or protection options after entering gameplay rather than at the title screen. Vehicle protection is intended for travel through hazardous space and should be disabled when testing normal damage behavior.

### Tools & Inventory

Collect or place the desired item in the inventory before using quantity-related options. Resource multipliers are best activated before scavenging. Avoid changing quest-critical items or important story objects, and disable inventory editing before transferring items between containers.

### World & Environment

Breathedge 2 centers on exploring hostile space, scavenging wreckage, gathering resources, crafting equipment, managing a crew, and piloting vehicles across different regions. Unlimited oxygen and vehicle protection are especially useful for extended expeditions, storms, unstable gravitational areas, and other environmental threats.

### Combat & Weapons

Enter an encounter before activating combat options. Enhanced weapon damage speeds up fights against hostile machines and creatures, while player protection prevents damage from enemy attacks and hazardous battle zones. Turn combat options off when you want to restore the game's intended challenge.
